Rooted in Rhythm is a cultural exchange model that honors and strengthens the shared cultural heritage of New Orleans and the Caribbean by cultivating cross-regional artistic collaboration, embedding wellness-centered practices in creative development, and positioning cultural exchange as an activator for community healing and economic growth.

From Congo Square to the Caribbean

Launch Party In New Orleans

Rooted in Rhythm and our partners presented a collaborative initiative: From Congo Square to the Caribbean. This cultural exchange artist residency connects artists of the African diaspora from New Orleans, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and Jamaica through creativity, wellness, and participation in community-led climate initiatives.
